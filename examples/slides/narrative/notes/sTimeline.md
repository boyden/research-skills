PATTERN: timeline / study design. Use it to define the time axis of a survival analysis (time zero, exposure window, event, censoring) or to show project phases. Three to six milestones.

Design rules shown: one horizontal axis with an arrow head; round markers on the axis, filled accent for the two that define the endpoint (time zero and event), white with a gray ring for the rest; labels alternate above and below so they never collide; one icon per milestone from one icon source, all the same size; a thin gray stem joins each marker to its label.

Numbers: 686 patients and 299 recurrence-or-death events from lifelines load_gbsg2; the timeline itself is the illustrative study's design, not GBSG2's.

Icons: Health Icons biopsy, tissue, hospital, regular-patient, chart-line (CC0).

Illustrative example: a design example, not a real study. Placeholders (n = XX) stand where a real deck would put numbers from its own result tables.
