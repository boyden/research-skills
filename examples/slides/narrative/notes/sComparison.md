PATTERN: two-column comparison (prior approaches vs this work, or pros vs cons). Use it when the audience needs the difference on a few named criteria, not a literature review.

Design rules shown: one row per criterion with the criterion name on the left, so the two columns are read across, not down; markers drawn from native shapes (accent check = has it, gray cross = lacks it, gray dash = partly) instead of symbol-font glyphs that may be missing on another machine; 'This work' column on a tint band with an accent header; rows separated by thin rules. Be honest: show where prior work is better (validation here) and say so in the take-home line.

Illustrative example: a design example, not a real study. Placeholders (n = XX) stand where a real deck would put numbers from its own result tables.
