PATTERN: key takeaways. Use it as the last content slide: three numbered messages the audience should leave with, each backed by one line of evidence from an earlier slide.

Design rules shown: accent numerals (44 pt) as the only color; each takeaway a short bold claim in black, then one gray evidence line; thin rules between rows; no conclusion box, because the takeaways are the conclusion. No number may appear here that was not on an earlier result slide, and the wording keeps the earlier qualifiers (candidate, exploratory).

Illustrative example: a design example, not a real study. Placeholders (n = XX) stand where a real deck would put numbers from its own result tables.
