PATTERN: section divider. Use it between major blocks of a long talk (20 minutes or more) as a pause; in a short talk the repeated agenda slide does the same job.

Design rules shown: no topic title, nothing to read except the number and the section name (the same words as on the agenda); one accent numeral, a thin gray rule, white space. The page number stays.

Illustrative example: a design example, not a real study. Placeholders (n = XX) stand where a real deck would put numbers from its own result tables.
